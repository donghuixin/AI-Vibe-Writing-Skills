# Paper Appreciation

Read [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) for workspace and scope conventions, then use
[18 · Paper Appreciation](../../.ai_context/prompts/18_paper_appreciation_agent.md).

Work from the supplied paper version and reading purpose. Record actual access and
verification scope, explain strengths with locations and reasons, distinguish quality
from missing evidence, and extract conditional lessons rather than personal style labels.
Use the linked rubric and template only to the depth the task needs.

Keep source documents unchanged unless revision was requested. Store notes only when
authorized and only in the paper workspace. Anonymized public output must not contain
the private source mapping. Appreciation does not imply reproduction or acceptance.
